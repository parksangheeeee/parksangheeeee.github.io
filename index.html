About Me

I am an AI Research Engineer at NAVER Cloud and a Ph.D. student at KAIST Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI, advised by Prof. Kee-Eung Kim at the Artificial Intelligence & Probabilistic Reasoning (AIPR) Lab. My long-term goal is to build multimodal AI that perceives and reasons about the world the way humans do — systems that go beyond processing pixels and tokens, and instead understand context, intent, and uncertainty. Toward this, I work on the vision capabilities of HyperCLOVA X, NAVER's multimodal large language model, while exploring how probabilistic reasoning can make multimodal systems more robust, reliable, and grounded in the real world. I am driven by the belief that AI should become not just more capable, but more trustworthy — genuinely useful to the people who live and work alongside it. My prior work spans document understanding, OCR, and natural language processing, built on a foundation in applied statistics and data science.
